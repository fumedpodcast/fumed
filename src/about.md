Team Bios

-David: David Leffler is a staff writer and investigative journalist at Public Health Watch who covers toxic chemical pollution in Texas. 

-Susan: Susan White has edited Pulitzer Prize-winning projects at InsideClimate News, ProPublica and the San Diego Union-Tribune. She has also edited two podcasts: Room 20 for the LA Times Studios and Border City for the Union-Tribune.

-Jordan: Jordan Gass-Pooré is an award-winning climate journalist and podcaster. She’s the creator and host of the podcasts “Hazard NJ” and “Hazard NYC,” which examine serious pollution issues. 

-Salina: Salina Arredondo is a Washington-based podcast producer and investigative journalist with in-depth knowledge about petrochemical barge regulation.

-Savanna: Savanna Strott is a staff writer and investigative journalist at Public Health Watch who specializes in data analysis and government accountability.

-Jana: Jana Cholakovska is an investigative reporter covering the environment, climate, and public health.  

-Mark: Mark Bush is a professional audio engineer, sound designer, musician, and composer with a diverse background in audio production and music performance. 

-Michael: Michael Ramos is a Grammy-winning musician, producer, and composer.

-Jim: Jim Morris is the founder of Public Health Watch and has been a journalist since 1978, focusing on public health and the environment.
